Our Mission

Policy that supports the most good for the most people.

At the Utilitarian Foundation, we recognize the need for policy that supports the most amount of good for the most amount of people.

We believe that the five key pillars of a strong government are social-democratic regulation of the economy, environmental protection, decriminalization, stronger education systems, and supporting political acceleration.

Five Pillars

What we build policy on.

Every position we take and every report we publish is anchored to one or more of these five foundations of strong, effective government.

Economic Regulation

Social-democratic oversight of markets that keeps growth broadly shared.

Environmental Protection

Safeguarding shared natural resources for current and future generations.

Decriminalization

Reducing unnecessary criminal penalties in favor of proportionate justice.

Stronger Education

Investing in public systems that expand opportunity from the ground up.

Political Acceleration

Moving durable, well-designed reform through government faster.
