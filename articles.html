Scylla's Data Centers: The Incredible Water Consumption of Data Centers in California, Why It Matters, and How to Manage It

California's ability to secure its economic and sustainability interests in the expanding data center market depends on holistic legislation surrounding water usage. This report lays out a two-pronged approach — statewide reporting mandates paired with incentives for co-investment in recycled water — to build public confidence while sustaining economic growth.
